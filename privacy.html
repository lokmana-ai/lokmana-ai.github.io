Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

In short: when you chat with a business that uses a Lokmana AI chatbot, we process your WhatsApp number, display name and messages only to answer you and pass your request to that business's staff. We do not sell your data, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

1. Who we are

Lokmana AI is an AI automation service based in Makassar, Indonesia. We build and operate chatbots on WhatsApp and on websites for hotels, restaurants and other service businesses ("client businesses"). This policy covers personal data processed by those chatbots and by this website.

When you message a client business, that business decides what the chatbot is used for. Lokmana AI processes your data on its behalf and under its instructions.

2. Data we collect

  • Contact details: your WhatsApp phone number and the profile name shown on WhatsApp.
  • Message content: the messages you send to the chatbot and the replies it sends back.
  • Booking or enquiry details you choose to share: for example your name, check-in and check-out dates, room type and number of guests.
  • Technical data: message IDs, timestamps and delivery status provided by the WhatsApp Business Platform.

We do not ask for payment card details, identity documents or other sensitive data through the chatbot. Please do not send them.

3. How we use it

  • To understand your question and reply to you.
  • To hand the conversation over to the client business's staff when you ask for a person, when the chatbot cannot answer, or when a booking needs to be confirmed.
  • To keep short-term context so the chatbot can follow the conversation.
  • To fix errors and improve the chatbot's answers.

We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

4. Services that process the data

To run the chatbot we use a small number of service providers, who process data only to provide their service:

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform): delivers messages between you and the chatbot.
  • OpenAI: generates the chatbot's replies from your message and the client business's information.
  • Hostinger: hosts the server that runs our automation software (n8n).
  • The client business you are messaging: its staff can read the conversation in order to help you.

Some of these providers store data outside Indonesia, for example in the United States, the European Union or Asia.

5. How long we keep it

Conversation context used by the chatbot is held in temporary memory only as long as needed to follow the conversation. Technical logs used for troubleshooting are kept for up to 90 days and then deleted. Booking details passed to a client business are kept by that business under its own policies.

6. Security

Access keys and credentials are stored encrypted on our server, and access to conversation data is limited to Lokmana AI and the relevant client business. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

7. Your rights and data deletion

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or to stop the chatbot from replying to you. To make a request:

  • Email lokmana.agency@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request", or
  • Message us on WhatsApp at +62 811-4447-990.

Please include the WhatsApp number you used and the name of the business you chatted with. We will respond within 14 days.

8. Children

Our chatbots are intended for adults making enquiries or bookings. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
