Avira Hotel Makassar

Two AI chatbots for one hotel. On WhatsApp, the bot answers guest questions and takes room reservations in the chat — room type, package, check-in and check-out dates — saving every booking to Google Sheets. On the website, a second chatbot handles guest enquiries and logs them in Airtable. Either way, the front office gets an instant email alert, so they can close OTA allotment before it overbooks.
